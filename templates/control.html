BOOTH OPERATIONS & RECOVERY

REBOOT SYSTEM?

The Raspberry Pi will reboot. The connection will drop for about 45-60 seconds while the kiosk restarts.

SAFE SHUTDOWN

This safely halts system processes and powers off the Pi. Wait 10 seconds after screen turns black before unplugging power.

DISABLE SERVICE & REBOOT?

This will disable the kiosk service and reboot the Pi directly to a standard Linux console login prompt.

The web dashboard will go offline until manually restored via SSH.
